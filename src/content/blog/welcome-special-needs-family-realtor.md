I'm Rachel Porter, a REALTOR® with Keeton & Co Real Estate in Glen Allen, and I've been helping families buy and sell homes across Central Virginia since 2017.

I'm also a single mom to two boys. My son Wyatt has special needs, and life with him has changed how I think about what "the right house" means.

## Why this focus

[Rachel: share in your own words what you've learned as Wyatt's mom: the things a listing never tells you, and what you wish someone had told you.]

For a lot of families, the questions that matter most aren't on the listing sheet:

- Can we get in the front door without steps?
- Is there a bedroom and full bath on the main level?
- How long is the drive to school, therapy, and the doctors we see every week?
- Is the street quiet? Is the yard safe and fenced?

Those are the questions I start with.
